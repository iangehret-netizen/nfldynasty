# NFL Dynasty

A phone-first NFL franchise game. Start with any of the 32 teams in any season from 2009 to 2026 as head coach (with or without GM powers), coordinator or position coach.

## Run it
- **GitHub Pages:** put this folder in a repository, turn on Pages for the branch, and open the site on your phone. "Add to Home Screen" installs it like an app (icons and manifest are included).
- **Locally:** open `index.html` in a browser.

Saves are kept in your browser (three slots). Use More › Saves › Download a backup to move a dynasty between devices.

## What's real
Week-by-week rosters, every draft from 2009 to 2026 plus undrafted signings, contracts, schedules, stadiums, roof, temperature and wind, final scores, head coaches, real trades, injuries and suspensions, combine results and season stats from 1999 on. Data comes from the open nflverse project. Team logos are embedded (nflverse squared logos).

## Where data was filled in
- Coordinators come from a hand-built list; a few recent staff slots are unknown and get generated names.
- College stats on prospect cards are estimated from ratings.
- Washington shows a plain badge for 2009 to 2021; other teams use their current logo in older seasons.
- Some older weekly rosters are incomplete; teams are topped up to 53 with real free agents first.
- Combine numbers are estimated where a prospect has no recorded result.
- Draft classes after 2027 come from real high-school recruiting classes, then from generated players.
- Career totals before 1999 are approximate.
